# Twilight Forest — Conquer a Magical Forest Dimension

Twilight Forest 9minecraft Mod adds a huge new dimension filled with giant trees, dark dungeons, and tough enemies. This mod turns the game into a quest where you explore magical biomes and fight your way through a set path of boss battles. You gather special loot, craft strong armor, and meet lots of new animals while wandering under a sky that always looks like sunset.

Boss Progression: You must defeat bosses in a specific order to unlock new areas of the map safely.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Benimatic**, **Team Twilight**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
